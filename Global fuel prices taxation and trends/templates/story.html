Story Narrative Introduction

Why does a liter of petrol cost five to ten times more in one country compared to another, even when both purchase crude oil from the same global market? This Tableau Story provides an empirical journey through global fuel prices, isolating the impact of international benchmarks (Brent Crude USD) from national fiscal decisions, sovereign subsidy shields, and economic development levels.
