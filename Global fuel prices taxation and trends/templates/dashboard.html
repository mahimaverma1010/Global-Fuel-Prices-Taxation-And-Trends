Global Fuel Analytics Dashboard

Interactive Tableau Public dashboard for evaluating global fuel price variations, taxation shares, and regional trends.

Open on Tableau Public
How to connect your own Tableau Dashboard:

Publish your workbook to Tableau Public, click the Share button on your visualization, copy the link, and paste it into the TABLEAU_CONFIG["DASHBOARD_URL"] setting inside app.py. The application will automatically embed your published visualization below.
