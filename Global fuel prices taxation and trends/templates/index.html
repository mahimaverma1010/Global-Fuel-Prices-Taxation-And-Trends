Project Scope & Purpose

Understanding Global Energy Economics

Retail fuel prices vary dramatically around the world, influenced not only by crude oil prices but also by domestic taxes, regional subsidies, and national income levels.

01
Crude Price Transmission

Examine how international Brent Crude oil price fluctuations transmit to national retail prices across different economies.

02
Taxation Disparities

Analyze how excise duties, environmental levies, and VAT differentiate consumer retail fuel costs from base refinery values.

03
Subsidy Mechanisms

Compare sovereign subsidy policies between oil-producing economies and net energy-importing nations across different time horizons.

04
Income & Regional Patterns

Evaluate the purchasing power impact and affordability differences across World Bank income classifications and geographical regions.

Analytical Pillars

Fuel Types & Fiscal Policy Overview

Key focus areas examined through the Tableau Public interactive visualizations.

Personal Mobility

Petrol (Gasoline)

Subject to the highest variance in retail taxation globally. High-income OECD nations often levy heavy carbon taxes and excise duties, whereas energy-rich nations maintain artificially depressed pump prices.

Logistics & Industry

Automotive Diesel

The backbone of commercial freight, shipping, and agricultural supply chains. Many emerging economies apply preferential tax rates or strategic price caps to control domestic food and freight inflation.

Residential & Clean Cooking

LPG (Liquefied Gas)

A critical household commodity directly linked to clean cooking transition programs and targeted social welfare transfers in developing and emerging markets.

Taxation & Subsidies
